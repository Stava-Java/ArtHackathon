# Xylophone samples

The WAV files in this directory are the medium-mallet Xylophone sound bank from
[FreePats](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ChromaticPercussion/xylophone.html), made
from the Versilian Community Sample Library by Versilian Studios LLC.

They are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

FreePats sound-bank version: 2020-07-06.
